About TechSmart

Technology training for confidence, well-being, and inclusion.

Our Mission

To improve the health and well-being of seniors through patient technology training.

Our Vision

Every senior feels confident, connected, and capable with technology.

Our Values

Compassion, respect, and inclusion.

TechSmart for Seniors is a nonprofit serving greater Atlanta. It began in 2012 as BlueHair Technology Group and teaches older adults how to use phones, tablets, and everyday apps.
