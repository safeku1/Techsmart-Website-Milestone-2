Technology That Helps You Stay at Home

October 8, 2026 · UCLA Health

  • Most adults over 50 want to stay in their own home. Simple tools can help.
  • A phone or tablet can set medicine reminders, make video calls, and read text out loud.
  • A voice helper can turn lights on, play music, or call a family member without small buttons.
  • Start with one tool you will actually use. You do not need a whole smart house.
