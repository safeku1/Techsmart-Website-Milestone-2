Online Safety for Older Adults

October 11, 2026 · First National Bank of Oklahoma

  • More of our daily life is now on a phone or computer. That includes banking, shopping, and talking with family.
  • Use a different password for email and banking. Turn on updates so your phone and computer stay safer.
  • Do not click a link in a text that says your account is locked. Open the app yourself.
  • If something feels rushed or scary, stop and ask someone you trust before you pay or share a code.
