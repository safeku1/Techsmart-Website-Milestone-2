Healthy Screen Habits for Older Adults

May 22, 2026 · KSL.com

  • Screens are fine when they help you learn, call family, or look something up.
  • The trouble starts when the screen replaces walking, sleep, or time with people.
  • Put the phone down during meals and visits. Make the screen time count.
  • If your eyes feel tired, look across the room for a few seconds, then come back.
