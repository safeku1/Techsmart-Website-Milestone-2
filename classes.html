Classes

Technology classes designed with the needs of older adults in mind.

Learn Technology. Build Confidence.

You love to learn, but the rapid pace of new technology in our daily lives can sometimes make you feel left behind. TechSmart is your partner in learning to use the latest phones, gadgets, and apps so you can stay in the know while having fun!

Our mission is to provide the training, insight, and advice you need to stay connected, informed, fulfilled, and safe.

One-on-One Training

Register yourself or a loved one for a personalized 60-minute technology lesson. Each class is tailored to your experience, interests, and learning needs.

You get individual attention, time to ask questions, and a comfortable pace so nothing feels rushed.

Group Training Sessions

TechSmart offers group technology classes online and through partner centers across Metro Atlanta.

These sessions help older adults build practical digital skills in a welcoming environment while connecting and learning alongside others.
