A Smart Guide to Wearable Health Technology

September 1, 2026 · AARP

  • A wearable is a watch, band, or ring that tracks steps, sleep, or heart rate.
  • Some can call for help if you fall. That can give you and your family peace of mind.
  • The numbers are a guide, not a doctor. Look at the pattern over days, not one odd reading.
  • Before you buy, check the price, battery life, and who can see your health data.
