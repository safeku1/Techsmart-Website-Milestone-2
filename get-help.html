How getting help works

A simple request is enough. We will follow up and match you with the support that fits you best.

1

Tell us what you need

Use the form to share your name, how to reach you, and what you want to learn.

2

We follow up

A team member will contact you by phone or email to talk through next steps.

3

Learn with support

Join one-on-one training or a group workshop and learn at a comfortable pace.

What we can help with

Lessons focus on everyday technology that helps older adults stay independent and connected.

Stay in touch

Email, texting, video calls, and sharing photos with family and friends.

Use your devices

Smartphones, tablets, and computers, including settings that make them easier to use.

Stay safer online

Passwords, scams, and simple habits that help protect your information.

Common questions

Tap a question to read the answer. You can open one at a time or several at once.

Do I need to know a lot about technology first?

No. We start where you are. You can ask questions, go slowly, and practice until it feels comfortable.

Can a family member request help for me?

Yes. Choose “A family member or friend” on the form and tell us who the lessons are for.

Are classes in person or online?

TechSmart offers supportive lessons in Greater Atlanta, including partner locations and online options when they fit your needs.
