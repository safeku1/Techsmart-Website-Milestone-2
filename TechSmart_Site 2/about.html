About TechSmart

Technology training designed to promote confidence, well-being, and inclusion for seniors.

Our Mission

To improve the health and well-being of seniors and older adults through technology training.

We believe teaching older adults how to use today’s tools promotes a happier, healthier, and longer life by reducing isolation, supporting independence, and keeping minds active.

Our Vision

Every senior feels confident, connected, and capable with technology.

TechSmart works toward a Greater Atlanta where older adults can use phones, tablets, and everyday apps without fear, and stay close to the people and services that matter.

Our Values

Compassion, respect, and inclusion.

We teach at a patient pace, without jargon, in a supportive setting. Questions are welcome, and no one is left behind.

How we’re doing

TechSmart is a nonprofit serving Greater Atlanta. Since 2012, we have helped thousands of older adults learn, connect, and thrive.

Our Founder

Jane Ratliff, Founder

Jane Ratliff founded BlueHair Technology Group in 2012, inspired by her mother’s struggles with technology. Seeing that frustration showed a clear need for senior-focused technology education.

Under Jane’s leadership, the organization developed teaching methods for adults over 60, especially women: patient, jargon-free instruction in a supportive environment. For seven years, seniors gained digital independence, stronger family connections, and safer habits online.

Jane retired in 2019. Her vision continues as TechSmart for Seniors, with the same mission: making technology accessible and empowering for older adults.
