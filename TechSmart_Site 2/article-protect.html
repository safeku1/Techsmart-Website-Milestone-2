Protect Your Most Important Information

By TechSmart Admin · November 3, 2022

Fraudsters use email more than any other tactic to hack into your computer system. Look closely at the address where that “important” email came from.

Crooks send phony email messages to millions of people just like you every day, usually with urgent instructions or tantalizing downloads about a free vacation or a new software update. These emails either take you to a website that requires you to fill out personal information or download a document that hijacks your computer.

Before you click, pause and check the sender’s address. If a message feels rushed, unexpected, or too good to be true, do not enter personal information or open the attachment. That simple habit can help protect your most important information.
