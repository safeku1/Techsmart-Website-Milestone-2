Protect Your Online Privacy

October 1, 2026 · Senior Planet from AARP

  • Many older adults are not sure how to keep personal information private online.
  • Check which apps can see your photos, location, and contacts. Turn off access you do not need.
  • Be careful what you type into an AI tool. Do not share your full address, bank details, or medical numbers.
  • Senior Planet has free classes and a short checklist if you want help with these steps.
